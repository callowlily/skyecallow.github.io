About

Skye Callow is an artist based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. She primarily works with photography and lens-based practices. Her work investigates aesthetics and the ways in which form generates and communicates meaning in images, particularly in relationship to place. Skye’s recent work experiments with the deconstruction, transformation, and translation of images through processes such as image transfer, fragmentation, and assemblage. She is fascinated by film grain and the digital pixel, and often utilizes both analog and digital photographic processes. Her practice is grounded in somatic experience and photographic theory, currently fueled by the writings of Hito Steyerl, Timothy Morton, and Tim Carpenter. Skye is interested in images as recordings, constructions, and as material objects. Her practice often expands the medium of photography beyond the 2D plane and towards the sculptural.

Skye is currently an MFA candidate at the University of Manitoba’s School of Art. She is originally from Sandy Hook, Manitoba, along the shores of Lake Winnipeg. Skye is the Distribution and Collections Coordinator at the Winnipeg Film Group and a member of the Open City Cinema Collective (The Winnipeg Underground Film Festival).
